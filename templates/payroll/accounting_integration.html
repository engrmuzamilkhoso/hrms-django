Accounting Integration

Connect your HRMS payroll to Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero, or a custom ERP via webhook. Map salary components to ledger account codes.

How it works

1. Configure the webhook endpoint of your accounting system.

2. Map each HRMS payroll component to a ledger account code.

3. After approving a payroll run, push the journal entry with one click.

4. The system sends a structured JSON payload — your accounting system records the entry automatically.
